AnyCareer: The Custom Career Creator

Features, FAQ & User Guide

Required: the latest version of the midnitetech_modlibrary.
About AnyCareer

AnyCareer is a fully Base Game compatible mod that lets you create the career of your dreams for your Sims.

Name it anthing you like, assign a description and icon, set a workplace name, schedule, pay & more!

To get started, you just need the mod itself and the latest version of my

📁modlibrary

Then, head to

📱Phone → Business → Find a Job
💻 Computer → Career → Find a Job
and choose AnyCareer: The Custom Career Creator

Setup

As soon as you join the career, you'll be automatically taken through the setup process.

Type in a name for your career, or click Randomize for some inspiration! Then, optionally add a Workplace name and description, and choose an Icon for your career.

I've included over 400 offical icons, and a few from The Sims 3, too! Once you've added the basic details, you can then choose your Schedule, and Hourly Pay.

When you've completed the setup, you can use your new career right away, or enable Work-From-Home, and read more About Work Preferences.

Any time you want to change anything, just head to a Computer or Mailbox, choose your career and Edit Details. You can also assign a Uniform here.

Work-From-Home

Enable this option from Computer → Career → Your Career → Add/Remove Work Preferences, or a Mailbox, to allow your Sim to be offered assignments from their workplace instead of heading to the office.

To personalize your Work-From-Home assignments, and Daily Tasks, you can set Work Preferences that suit your career.

Work Preferences

Under Computer → Career → Your Career → Add/Remove Work Preferences, or a Mailbox, you'll find options to customize your work experience.

  • Add an Employment Role to specify your Sim's job responsibilities, i.e., Employee, Owner, etc. This option will help make Daily Tasks more specific.

If you've enabled Work-From-Home, you can also select from the following options. 

  • Work Relationships: Choose the type of Sims you work with, i.e., Clients, Customers, Patients, etc.

  • Work Tasks: Add up to 3 Work Tasks from 6 categories, like Business & Administration and Care & Services to offer a better variety of assignments and dictate what kind of work you'll do.

  • Work Venues: Optionally add a Venue where you'll work. For example, choose the Library, and you'll be offered active style assignments. You'll travel to the Library, Clock In, and work through different tasks, like Chatting with Patrons, Assisting with Registration, etc.

The more Work Preferences you add, the better suited your assignments will be to that Sim. You can View & Remove Work Preferences from the Career menu on a Computer or from a Mailbox at any time.

Promotions & Objectives

When your Sim is promoted to Level 02, they'll be able to choose a Preferred Skill. You can choose any, including Custom Skills if you have any installed. This skill will then be used for their Promotion Objectives.

You'll also be prompted to Update your Sim's title if you like, or choose Don’t Ask Again to stick with their current one. You can opt out of Promotions at any time.

Their Salary will increase with each promotion, but you can adjust the amounts whenever you like.

Promotions also include monetary rewards.

FAQ

How do I change my title?

You can change your title, description, and other details from the Edit (Your Career) Details menu on a Computer or Mailbox.

Do I have to use the Work Preferences? I just want a basic career.

No, they're totally optional. They just help to personalize your Sim's assignments if they opt to Work-From-Home.

Does the career have Daily Tasks?

Yes, the career includes Daily Tasks that your Sim can complete each day. These can be personalized by choosing an Employment Role.
